# Founders NFT Tokenomics

## Founders NFT

### Supply

- **Total Supply**: 100
- **Price per NFT**: $10,000 USDC
- **Total Raise**: $1,000,000

### Revenue Share

Founders collectively receive **8%** of gross platform revenue.

- Distributed **weekly** via on-chain airdrops
- Airdrops occur every **Sunday at 00:00 UTC**
- Each Founder NFT receives **0.8%** of total platform revenue

## Revenue Share

Founders collectively receive **8%** of gross platform revenue.

- Distributed **weekly** via on-chain airdrops
- Airdrops occur every **Sunday at 00:00 UTC**
- Each Founder NFT receives **0.8%** of total platform revenue

## Revenue Sources

- League entry fees (primary)
- Gacha sales
- Future platform fees

## Distribution

- Revenue is calculated weekly based on league activity
- Airdrops are pushed automatically to all 100 holders
- No staking is required to receive distributions

## Example

If the platform generates **$500,000** in revenue in a given week:

- Total to Founders: **$40,000**
- Per Founder NFT: **$400**

---

## Founders Tokens

The platform will also have a separate **Founders Token** with the following initial parameters:

- **Total Supply**: 20,000
- **Developer Allocation**: 60% (12,000 tokens)
- **Liquidity / Early Sale**: 40% (8,000 tokens)

The 40% allocation will be used to seed liquidity through Solana/USDC purchases. The developer intends to remain a long-term holder.
